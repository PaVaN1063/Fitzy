How this is calculated

  • BMR (Mifflin–St Jeor): 10 × weight + 6.25 × height − 5 × age, +5 for men or −161 for women.
  • Maintenance = BMR × activity factor.
  • Target = maintenance −20% to lose fat, +10% to build muscle (never below 1,500 kcal for men or 1,200 for women).
  • Fat = 25% of calories (at least 0.6 g per kg). Carbs fill the rest.
  • Water = 35 ml per kg, +500 ml on gym days.
